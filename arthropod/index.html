SMALL STEPS, COMPLEX MOTION

A body that listens
to its feet.

This creature is animated in real time. Each step responds to the surface beneath it.

01

Find a foothold

Feet sample the terrain ahead, then stay planted while the body moves.

02

Share the balance

Two alternating groups of three legs lift and land together. Joint angles are solved with inverse kinematics.

03

Follow the surface

The body turns with the terrain, even onto a wall. Adhesion is a procedural constraint, rather than a full physical simulation.
